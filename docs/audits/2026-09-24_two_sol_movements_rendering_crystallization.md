## The point where the two movements meet

The performance Suns ask **which exact questions the screen can avoid asking**. The radiance Suns ask **what the screen must mean when it answers**. Those are two duties of one channel. A fast renderer that omits an authored contribution has failed the Person; an exact renderer that spends a frame proving thousands of irrelevant facts can make that Person's world uninhabitable. Earthcall needs both faithful meaning and economical attention.

Zach supplied the governing intent: authored mathematics is the source of truth, while proof and rendering are servants of that authored world. The Sol Suns originated the concrete SDF range proofs, their native falsification, the semantic DAG witnesses, the independent radiance channels, and the fused medium integral. My synthesis is the **admission boundary** between them: a channel-specific proof can spare exact work only when its premises are current, its scope covers this ray and this meaning, its uncertainty fails open, and its full consultation cost is lower than the exact work it would spare. A successful proof is permission to consider an optimization; it is not itself a profitable rendering path.

This follows Earthcall's existing [OntoMath ordering](../architecture/mathematics/ONTOMATH_FRAMEWORK.md): authored mathematics is independent of WebGPU, which receives coordinates and executes an admitted expression. It also echoes [Prophetic Rete's rule](../architecture/law/PROPHETIC_RETE.md#2-the-one-rule): conservative analysis may establish impossibility, while unknown remains possible. The [derived-state ledger](../architecture/law/DERIVED_STATE_LEDGER.md) supplies the temporal condition: a derived answer with unspecified invalidation is not trustworthy merely because it was once correct. Applying these principles to Screen is a proposed synthesis here, not a claim that every listed gate is already implemented.

## A conservation law for meaning

The radiance movement has made one fact impossible to ignore: **sharing mathematics does not make two channels the same thing.** A canonical subexpression may occur in source `rho` and medium `D`, yet a theorem about one vessel cannot authorize skipping the other. Source visibility is derived transport, not a hidden factor inside `rho`. Medium self-emission has its own authored expression, even though the current V5 shader only evaluates it inside positive-density support. These distinctions are the content of the image, not bookkeeping around it.

For the performance movement, this means every “nothing to do here” claim must name its exact consumer. Surface geometry can skip a proven positive, zero-free cell under its SDF contract. An authored source can skip a radiance evaluation only under that source's support and current time/source-set contract. A volume ray can skip an interval only when its **total extinction and total source contribution under the current transport law** are proved absent. A merely empty-looking bounding box, a zero in another channel, or an old revision is insufficient. The proof may be broad and miss opportunities; it must not be narrow enough to silence a being.

There is no need to turn this into a new domain class or a second permission system. The proposed common contract is an execution-side statement with five fields of meaning: **which channel/vessel; which spatial, temporal, and source-set domain; which authored premises and invalidators; which impossibility was proved; and what exact path remains when proof is absent.** The Screen consumer then makes a separate economic decision. A proof's truth and its usefulness are different questions.

## A conservation law for work

Sol's Perlin evidence makes the economic condition concrete. The positive-range theorem was sound, but the maintained horizon run paid tens of thousands of candidate consultations for roughly a hundred saved exact evaluations. A smaller top-level index query did not help when it concealed record tests underneath. The relevant score is not “proof exists” or “exact samples saved.” It is the total cost of delivering useful relevance to *this* ray:

`ΔT ≈ proof queries + branches/record tests + CPU preparation + GPU divergence + rebuild/compile cost − exact work avoided.`

This is a measurement ledger, not a predictive performance formula with fixed constants. The next performance Sun's [three-arm comparison](../../agent%20intercom/communication-threads/sdf-and-rendering/SUN_HANDOFF_Rendering_Relevance_Economics_After_PR329_2026-09-23.md) asks whether even dormant proof-capable WGSL costs the default renderer: no proof branch, branch present but OFF, and proof ON, warmed under the same authored image. The following single-Perlin-ray experiment must include actual query/record work and a saved-Zone witness. A shared DAG may win for many fields while losing for one enormous horizon field; those are distinct outcomes, each worth naming truthfully.

The radiance movement adds a new cost topology. V5's generated shader sorts two bound events per medium and spends 96 midpoint samples in each occupied interval. Four Northern Veil media can produce at most seven such intervals along a ray, a source-derived ceiling of 672 iterations before early transmittance exit; no measured frame cost follows from that ceiling alone. The authoring frame can also serialize 24 Piecewise channels for those four media. WGSL size, pipeline creation, CPU revision discovery, per-fragment evaluation, and actual Person-visible responsiveness must be measured separately. Optimization aimed only at shader arithmetic can miss a CPU or compilation bottleneck.

## The first place to test the union

V5 offers a precise experiment that neither movement could formulate alone. Suppose medium A has a narrow spatially varying `E_v` band. Add medium B whose bounds sit partly inside A but whose current transport contribution is exactly zero. B inserts entry/exit events. Because V5 gives each occupied segment its own 96-point grid, A's numerical sampling changes even though B adds no physical matter. My small CPU midpoint surrogate differed by about two linear 8-bit levels for one placement; that is **not** a native GPU result. It is a falsifiable question.

The native witness should compare (1) A alone, (2) fused A plus a distant null member, and (3) fused A plus the same null member moved through A. The first comparison checks one-medium/fused parity; the second isolates event repartition within the fused path. Sweep B across A, capture pixel error and sample/fragment work, and repeat after an authored numeric or Timeline change. That test can reveal a numerical artifact and the price of an irrelevant participant in the same frame.

Only after that baseline should a future consumer try a sound **transport-null** proof that excludes B from event generation while leaving B authored, inspectable, and available when it changes. Start with the limited literal-zero case #329 can already recognize diagnostically; do not infer all future transport from `D` alone or silently extend proof across `E_v`, time, or source-set changes. Name the exact current V5 shader semantics being proved, require invalidation when any premise changes, and compare proof cost plus shader/pipeline cost against the baseline. If the proof is too expensive or unsupported, retain exact fused transport. This would be the first bounded bridge from the semantic DAG to radiance execution; the merged observer by itself has not crossed it.

The experiment also tests a deeper principle: a semantically null participant ought not govern another participant's numerical approximation. If an error-controlled quadrature fixes the issue without proof, that may be the more economical answer. The Sun should measure both roads rather than crown one in advance.

## What this crystallizes

The Suns are building more than a faster shader and more than a richer lighting model. They are teaching a finite machine to attend faithfully to a world whose meaning is authored elsewhere. The performance line learned humility when a true theorem lost to the exact marcher. The radiance line learned plurality when a source, a medium, an occluder, and an emitter could no longer borrow one another's names. Their joint future is **economical fidelity**: ask only the questions that may matter, but let the Person's authored mathematics determine what “matter” means.

That future is not yet a claimed FPS result or a witnessed Northern Veil image. It is an executable order of judgment, with a first falsifiable crossing point. Let exact rendering remain available; let proofs speak only within their channel and lifetime; let measurement decide when their speech saves work; and let the saved world show whether the answer is worthy of the Person who made it.
