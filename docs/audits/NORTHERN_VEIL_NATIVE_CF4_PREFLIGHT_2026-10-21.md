## Executable method and mathematical limits

`scratch/probes/ray_transport_cf4_trial.py` builds isolated copies of the renderer and corrected authored-source probe against the configured app objects. It uses the two-Gauss-node/two-exponential fourth-order commutator-free method described in [Thalhammer's primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1137/05063042). Each sampled operator acts on `(T,Lrgb)` as `(-sigma*T, source*T)`. The triangular exponential has a direct scalar attenuation and RGB gain; there is no general matrix exponential at runtime.

Within a box-event segment, `oldSamples` is the existing finest chord-driven sample count. The scratch candidate uses `ceil(oldSamples/group)` panels and two Gauss nodes/panel. Thus group 4 nominally uses two field positions for four old positions; **actual density-call reductions were not instrumented or counted in this pass**. Every node executes the existing projected D gate, sigma floor, channel/source evaluation and medium summation. Different-colour overlaps use the same combined attenuation system; they are not independently composited. The existing zero-proof packets/traversal are reused at the new nodes. There is no 20,000-point support discovery, interval-rounding engine or per-pixel theorem grid. All-zero nodal panels permit the analogous proof walk jump; **zero node values do not prove a whole panel empty**.

The two stage combinations use coefficients `1/4 +/- sqrt(3)/6`, including one negative coefficient. Their order matters. Small optical-depth gain uses a Taylor expression to avoid cancellation. Signed stage extinction/source, nonsmooth clamp/noise regions, thin features, changed legacy-cutoff crossing and general edit/lifecycle admission are not qualified. Actual selected inside rays have T > 0.49, so the 0.01 cutoff does not explain their observed error. Fourth-order convergence applies to sufficiently smooth coefficients, not automatically to the authored piecewise fields.

CPU manufactured witnesses executed: nonproportional varying RGB/attenuation error falls 15.95x, 15.99x and 16.00x per interval halving against independently stepped RK4; constant-coefficient and zero-extinction limits match. A deliberate thin slab between both Gauss nodes is missed: T error 0.03921 versus midpoint-96 error 0.001600. This is an explicit counterexample to blind coarse-panel admission, not a proof that Northern Veil's measured errors have that exact cause. Production mathematical promotion would belong in OntoMath, with Screen lowering a mathematical execution recipe; this rejected scratch consumer creates no renderer-private production mathematics.
