This is one of the things that originally inspired me to create Earthcall in the first place.

Basically, you have to realize every single digital art program is essentially a locked-geometry thing with specific Laws about how pixels are applied.

Mathematical/gemoetry/modeling engines are precise things that can be authored in many ways but no matter how many ways its limited to the engines pre-specifications. New laws can't be authored at runtime. You're stuck to whatever the interface gives you—typing it parameters manualy, etc.. If you could modify it with the granularity and human-ness of a digital art studio, that would be incredible.

And physics. Realistic simulation is essentially the latter two but with real-time change instead of merely authoring static pictures.

So here's the thing. This is a triumvirate where each of the three individually is very impressive but is fundamentally limited by not having hte features of the others. If you had all three, you get something very close to a unified simulator of visual-mathematical phenomeonology. That is incredibly powerful because you aren't limited to just, like, having your beautiful artwork in one medium anymore. You can turn it into a movie. Make physics that are directly compatible with every element in the artwork at full fidelity. And for mathematical models. The granular hand-control that art engines often have but math engines lack is exactly what Earthcall's direct Sense-Act Person Singularity control gives (that is another mouthful bruh). Get the idea?  

Beautiy is also deeply matheamtical. Imagine having the limitless power and precision of a pure-math engine like Ontomath——like fractals, recursive sequences, topology, etc.. You could use this to build and explore unfathomable strctures and with the art hand-control capacities you can make this you could smooth it and sculpt it and mold it however you want to become one with your art creation.

Music too. Music no longer gets siloed inside composing softwares, recorders, musicician collaboration apps. You don't evne just have the "collaborations" between music and physics that video games or 8D audio often give. You have a complete interwoven unity on the level of Laws.
