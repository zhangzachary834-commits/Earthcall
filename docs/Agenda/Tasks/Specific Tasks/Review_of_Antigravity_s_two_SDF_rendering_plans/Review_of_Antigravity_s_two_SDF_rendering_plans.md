✅ **Review of Antigravity's two SDF rendering plans** — done (2026-08-31), [REVIEW_OF_ANTIGRAVITY_SDF_RENDERING_PLANS_2026-08-31.md](../../../../audits/REVIEW_OF_ANTIGRAVITY_SDF_RENDERING_PLANS_2026-08-31.md). Verdict in short: technique selection is good and **symbolic normal emission (manifold plan Phase 3) is the strongest unbuilt idea in the tree** — it removes three `sdfEval` calls per march step on the implicit path, which is the whole of Bugs.md #17, and the plan undersells it as a shading-only win. Two faults run through all three documents: **every bound is a picked number rather than a derived one** (600 units, 200-300 units, 1.5 units, 0.25 units, 64 m tiles), and `min(box.y, misc.z)` — a length compared against an eye-relative `t` — is Bugs.md #20; and **the falsifying measurement is scheduled after the work**, which is how a two-dozen-commit campaign went into a subsystem that was never the bottleneck. Also: expansion Phase A (swap `cnoise3` for a 64^3 RGBA8 texture) must not be built as written — it substitutes a different function for a Person's authored `Op::Noise` while the CPU keeps computing `glm::perlin`, which is the same class of defect as the simplex substitution already reverted; Phase D (subgroup compaction) is blocked on a prerequisite the plan never names, since `WebGpuContext.mm:82` requests a **null** device descriptor and subgroups are an opt-in native feature; and all three plans' interactive verification step names `./build/earthcall`, the **OpenGL** binary, where none of the WGSL they modify ever runs. Build order proposed in the review.
