**Person-read ledger** — mark, per framework doc, whether the world's author has personally read it. An agent-written doctrine the Person has not read is a *proposal*; it becomes doctrine when his eyes have been on it. A summary is a report authored by the being it reports on — the same shape as this repo's documented self-agreement failures — and the ledger is the structural counter: agents know which texts carry ratified authority, and "eventually read everything" becomes a visible, shrinking frontier.
