✅ **The change feed was broken for every non-`PropertyRef` property** — found and fixed 2026-09-01 while building the above; this is very likely the "we stopped one implementation step short" the *"Audit whether the Rete is actually skipping known facts"* item below was reaching for. `PropertyRef::set` was the **only** call site of `Singular::notifyPropertyChanged` in the entire engine. So `Object::position`/`rotation` (ComputedProperty over the transform matrix), all seven hand-written `Property` bridges in `ObjectProperties.cpp` (shape params, shape kind, field shapes, patch controls, rigid forms, **face colours**), every `Relation` property, and every **authored** property (`AddProperty`, in the dynamic map) never marked a fact dirty. A `WhileTrue` law watching `position.y` therefore matched only beings that *already* satisfied it when the network first met them and went permanently deaf afterwards — silently, with the law still registered, enabled and compiled and its alpha memory simply empty. Fixed at the two write seams rather than per Property subclass, so a new bridge cannot forget: `PropertyPath::setValue` (with `resolve` gaining an `owner` out-param, since the notification needs the Singular the property is registered *on*, not the root, once a path descends) and `Singular::setDynamicProperty`. **Still not caught, deliberately:** a direct C++ setter (`obj.setPosition(...)`) bypasses the property vocabulary entirely — always did; the per-frame world seeding is what keeps those from being lost.
