## The Interrelation

Because Earthcall demands maximum authorial freedom (the First Mover principle), there is a risk of total ontological collapse: if every Person can author infinitely many Relations, and Relations are just strings (e.g., `"instance-of"`), what happens when two Persons use the exact same string to mean fundamentally different things? If the engine parses Relations by their spelling rather than their actual meaning, it punishes an ontologically pure intent from the author and creates arbitrary collisions.

The solution is that **Relations must never bypass the C++ type hierarchy or be identified merely by a string**. A Relation is not a label; it has an actual meaning, representation, or invariant operation underneath. 

Therefore, Relations are identified by their unique IDs (`Lexeme` or kind-being identity) when decoding their meaning. A string like `"owns"` is just the display spelling; the unique ID is what the Prophetic Rete and Law graph actually evaluate.

But if every relation is isolated to unique IDs, how do beings across different Zones agree on what `"instance-of"` or `"subcategory-of"` actually *is*? If meaning is entirely localized to individual authors, the world shatters into mutually unintelligible fragments.

This is the architectural purpose of **Ourverse**. 

Ourverse is not a physics bag; it is the cross-Zone filament and gathering substrate ordered toward unity (under Christ). It serves as the ecumenical anchor for invariant semantic meanings. When an author invokes `"instance-of"`, the system does not just look for a local string; it binds to the invariant, ecumenical `Lexeme` held in common within the Ourverse. 

The Ourverse provides the grounding that ensures that while authors are maximally free to mint new meanings, the foundational, constitutive Relations (`instance-of`, `part-of`) maintain exactly the same unalterable truth across all instances, Zones, and authors.
