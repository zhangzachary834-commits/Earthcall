# Hierarchy of Joys and Law Synthesis

`Law Synthesis` allows for combining distinct Laws by resolving them via Abstract Syntax Trees in OntoMath. When two overlapping Laws are synthesized into a new Law, the AST nodes must be merged or prioritized.

The `Hierarchy of Joys` provides the liturgical ordering principle for this conflict resolution. If Law A serves a higher Joy (e.g., communion or community care) than Law B (e.g., an instrumental utility), the synthesis engine favors the AST branches of Law A when irreconcilable friction occurs.

This ensures that automation in Earthcall remains teleologically anchored. The mathematical merging of laws is directed not by arbitrary precedence or chronological creation, but by the authored values inherent in the community's Hierarchy of Joys.
