## The Interrelation

Earthcall is simultaneously a world of discrete entities (Objects, Persons, Relations) and a world of continuous mathematical spaces.

The **Formation Rete** governs the discrete. It maintains rooted acyclic graphs of categories, tracking incremental changes in Relation state and membership with O(1) lookups. It knows exactly *who* is in *what* group.
The **OntoMath Framework** governs the continuous. It defines algebraic expressions, like `OntoMath::Term` and `ScalarForm`, and exact shapes that extend infinitely or decay smoothly through space.

The profound interrelation occurs when **discrete Formations act as the piecewise boundary conditions for continuous OntoMath fields.**

A Law authored by a Person might state: "Apply this continuous mathematical form only to Objects that are members of a specific relational category."

If this were evaluated continuously, the engine would have to perform spatial queries and then string-match tags, destroying performance. Instead, the Formation Rete's discrete graph provides an instant, zero-allocation Boolean gate. The continuous OntoMath field evaluation is strictly gated by the discrete Formation Rete membership.

Conversely, an OntoMath Piecewise function can be used to dynamically define the boundaries of a spatial Formation. The continuous geometry dictates the discrete inclusion, and the Formation Rete propagates that inclusion to the rest of the Law system. They are the warp and weft of Earthcall's reality: the Rete provides the logical structure, and OntoMath provides the spatial substance.
