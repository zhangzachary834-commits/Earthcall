## The Interrelation

First Mover Authoring is the principle that a human (the First Mover) can write directly into Earthcall's reality, creating Objects, Laws, and Relations without relying on hardcoded C++ classes or engine-specific abstractions. OntoMath is the principle that the mathematical rules governing reality must be exact, symbolic, and distinct from any particular rendering or physics framework.

The profound interrelation between these two pillars is that **First Mover Authoring is the only mechanism that allows OntoMath to remain ontologically pure.**

If Earthcall relied on traditional game design tools, a human would place a "Wind Field" object, and the engine would map that to a black-box physics shader. In Earthcall, because of Refusal 6 (No black boxes), this is forbidden. Instead, the First Mover uses their authoring power to directly construct the math that defines the wind. They author the `ScalarForm` itself.

By doing so, the First Mover bridges the gap between subjective creative intent ("I want a swirling wind here") and objective mathematical reality. The First Mover doesn't write shaders; they author pure math into the serialization. The engine then reads this math and executes it. This makes the First Mover the direct author of reality's equations, ensuring that the underlying mathematics of the world are completely legible, legible to the First Mover, and free from engine-imposed illusions.
