## The Interrelation

Earthcall defines the **Ourverse** as the vessel of unity, containing **Gathering Zones** that are unowned, mutual spaces seeded with first-mover metalaws. These spaces are inherently ecumenical, where multiple Persons and objects converge.

Concurrently, the **Transfer Policy** (`Singularity/TransferPolicy`) governs the derivation, transfer, and access of properties. It gates set-to-set transfer and general path access, acting as the singular authority office for determining who can read, write, or derive a property.

The interrelation becomes critical when entities interact within a Gathering Zone. Because a Gathering Zone is unowned, there is no localized "Zone Owner" to unilaterally dictate the terms of interaction or override the Transfer Policy. Instead, the Transfer Policy must mediate interactions based on the individual relations and properties of the entities themselves, guided by the ecumenical metalaws of the space.

When a Person brings an authored Object into a Gathering Zone, the Transfer Policy ensures that the Object's properties remain sovereign to the author, unless explicitly shared or subjected to mutual Laws. The Transfer Policy does not yield to the Gathering Zone; rather, the Gathering Zone is defined by the fact that it respects the Transfer Policy without asserting ownership over the entities within it.

This creates a true shared space. If Person A attempts to modify Person B's object within the Gathering Zone, the Transfer Policy evaluates the request based on the Relations between A and B, not the zone itself. The Gathering Zone provides the dimensional and spatial context for the interaction, but the Transfer Policy enforces the structural integrity of authorship and governance, ensuring that "unity" does not dissolve into "unauthorized mutation."
