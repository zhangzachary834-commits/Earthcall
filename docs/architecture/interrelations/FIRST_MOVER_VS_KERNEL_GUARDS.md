## The Interrelation

Earthcall is built on the premise of radical authorial sovereignty. `FIRST_MOVER_AUTHORING.md` defines the First Mover as the absolute architect of a world's starting state. They define what things exist, what relations bind them, and what Laws govern them. The Engine generally acts merely as a faithful interpreter of this authored data.

However, this absolute authority of the First Mover collides directly with Earthcall's physical nature—specifically, its connection to the real human body via Modality Channels (like Audio and Screens).

If a First Mover authors a Law that generates dangerous infrasound or strobing light patterns known to induce seizures, should the Engine faithfully execute it, honoring the First Mover's authority?

The resolution of this conflict is found in the architectural split between **Ontology/Mathematics** and the **Modality Channels** (detailed in `ONTOMATH_FRAMEWORK.md` §7a).

The rule is: **The First Mover has absolute authority over the abstract mathematics of the world, but zero authority over the physical path to the human body.**

1. **The Math is Sovereign:** A First Mover can author an OntoMath AST representing a 7Hz wave. The Engine will flawlessly parse it, differentiate it, integrate it, and allow Laws to interact with it. In the abstract simulation, that 7Hz wave exists perfectly.
2. **The Guard is Absolute:** When that 7Hz wave attempts to leave the simulation and enter the human ear via the Audio Channel, the hardcoded C++ Kernel Guard intercepts it. The Audio Channel refuses to render the sound buffer, logging a violation.

The First Mover cannot author a Law to override this guard because the guard is not a Law; it is a C++ constraint built into the substrate. It is not a setting to be toggled, but a physical limitation of the Engine's connection to reality.

**Conclusion:** The interrelation between First Mover authoring and Kernel Guards establishes that while reality *within* Earthcall is entirely malleable and subject to human intention, the bridge between Earthcall and the physical world is constrained by immutable physical safety limits. You can imagine a deadly sound, but you cannot force the Engine to play it.
