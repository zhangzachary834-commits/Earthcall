## The Interrelation

The "Hierarchy of Joys" specifies that the ultimate meaning or purpose (telos) of actions and beings within Earthcall is not a vague concept, nor a hidden C++ enumeration. It is a concrete `Formation` of `Lexeme`s.

But representing telos as data is only half the requirement. If that data were sequestered in a private `Person::_telosRoot` member, it would fail the fundamental design criteria of Earthcall.

Because of the "No Black Box" doctrine, this telos Formation must be explicitly registered as a property. This seemingly minor technical requirement—exposing the `joyOrdering` or `telos` pointer to the `Singularity`'s property paths—has profound ontological implications.

It means **telos is a physical property of the world.**

Just as a Law can check an Object's `temperature` and ignite it, a Law can check a Person's current `telos` property.

This interconnects the philosophical architecture with the mechanical one:
1. Because `telos` is not a black box, a Law can be authored to change the environment *based* on a Person's current highest Joy.
2. Because `telos` is governed by the same `TransferPolicy` gates as any other property, a Person has exact, cryptographically sound sovereignty over who or what is allowed to alter or observe their stated intentions.

**Conclusion:** "No Black Box" forces the "Hierarchy of Joys" out of the realm of abstract metadata and into the tactile, governable physics of the engine. Intention becomes a physical dimension that Laws can interact with, strictly bounded by the Person's own authority.
