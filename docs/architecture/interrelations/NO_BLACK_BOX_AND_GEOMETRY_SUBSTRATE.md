## The Interrelation

"No Black Box" mandates that every property of a being must be registered, exposed, and legible to the Law system. However, rendering graphics and simulating collisions require hyper-optimized, low-level data structures (like SdfOps and topological discriminants) that are inappropriate for Persons to author directly.

The "Geometry Execution Substrate" resolves this tension by defining a hard boundary: low-level execution tags operate *beneath the Kernel* as machine mechanism. They are explicit instructions to the engine on how to lower and evaluate form, not claims about what a thing *is*. Because the manifesto strictly prohibits these execution tags from flowing upward to become authored meaning, they do not violate "No Black Box." The Person authors legible meaning (which is fully exposed), and the engine lowers it into opaque execution structures that are explicitly exempted from ontological legibility.
