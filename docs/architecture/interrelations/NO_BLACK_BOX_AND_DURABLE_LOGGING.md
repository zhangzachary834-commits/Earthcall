# No Black Box and Per-Singular Durable Logging

**How the imperative for legibility demands a permanent, per-being historical record.**

## The Interrelation

The "No Black Box" principle demands that the human intent behind any behavior in Earthcall is fully legible and not hidden behind opaque C++ execution.

However, legibility of intent is insufficient if the *execution* of that intent is lost the moment a process ends. "Per-Singular Durable Logging" provides the required temporal dimension to the No Black Box principle.

By routing semantic events to stable, identity-aware historical files rather than a monolithic transient console stream, Durable Logging ensures that the engine remains a transparent glass box not just during the present instant, but across the entire lifespan of a Singular being.
