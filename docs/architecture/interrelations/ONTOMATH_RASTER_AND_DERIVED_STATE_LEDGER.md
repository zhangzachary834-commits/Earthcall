## The Interrelation

OntoMath Raster Formations describe how opaque foreign data (like PNGs or HTML DOM) is ingested into continuous, mathematically exact OntoMath fields. However, these fields represent a derived state: their true source of truth is the ingested asset or DOM tree, but their runtime manifestation is a generated mathematical field.

The Derived-State Ledger mandates that any generated structure must declare its invalidation conditions—who notices when it goes wrong, and what dependencies trigger its rebuild. If an OntoMath Raster Formation fails to link its generated continuous field to the lifecycle of the underlying raster or HTML node, it violates the ledger and becomes an un-governed black box. Thus, the exact algebraic AST generated by the OntoMath Raster system must be explicitly registered as derived state, ensuring that if the source image or DOM node mutates, the engine deterministically invalidates and re-derives the associated OntoMath field.
