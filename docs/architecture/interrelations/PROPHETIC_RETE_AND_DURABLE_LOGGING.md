# Prophetic Rete and Per-Singular Durable Logging

**How ahead-of-time collision detection integrates with permanent historical observability.**

## The Interrelation

The Prophetic Rete predicts state mutations and detects conflicts before they occur. When it detects a collision that violates the Hierarchy of Joys, it pre-emptively refuses the execution of the lower-priority Law.

Per-Singular Durable Logging requires that refusals are first-class historical events. A Law that refuses to execute must have that refusal recorded in its durable history, and the beings involved must have a record of why the state transition was denied.

Therefore, the Prophetic Rete acts as a primary source of semantic refusal events for the Durable Logging system. Without the logger, the Rete's adjudications are ephemeral console ghosts; without the Rete, the logger misses the critical "why" behind unchanged state.
