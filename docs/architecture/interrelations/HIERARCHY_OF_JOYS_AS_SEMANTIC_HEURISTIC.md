## The Interrelation

The `SEMANTIC_NETWORK_VISION.md` outlines an architecture where the engine subconsciously infers new semantic relations based on existing facts (e.g., if a Sword belongs to Arthur, and Arthur is in the Castle, the engine infers the Sword is in the Castle).

However, uncontrolled semantic inference creates a catastrophic combinatorial explosion. If every possible logical deduction is instantiated as a new `Relation` being, the engine would instantly exhaust memory generating billions of trivial facts (e.g., "The speck of dust on the sword is in the castle," "The sword is near the wall," "The sword is not an apple"). Traditional semantic web systems rely on strict, hardcoded search depths and relevance weighting algorithms to prune this explosion.

Earthcall refuses hardcoded, black-box pruning algorithms. Instead, the heuristic pruning of semantic inference is driven by **Telos**, specifically through the `HIERARCHY_OF_JOYS.md`.

Because Telos (the highest intended Joy or purpose of a Person at a given moment) is exposed as a physical, introspectable property path (`TELOS_AS_A_PHYSICAL_PROPERTY.md`), it can be queried by the very Laws that generate semantic relations.

When a semantic inference Law triggers, it does not blindly instantiate every logically valid conclusion. Instead, the Law checks the current `telos` of the relevant Person or Zone. The inference is only committed to reality if the resulting `Relation` is aligned with or relevant to that Telos.

If a Person's current highest Joy is "Forging" (a specific `Lexeme`), the inference Law might instantiate relations about the metallurgical properties of the Sword and its proximity to the anvil, while completely ignoring the Sword's relationship to the Castle's architecture.

**Conclusion:** The Hierarchy of Joys is not just a philosophical mission statement; it is the fundamental mathematical heuristic that makes a subconscious semantic network computable. By bounding inference to human intention, the engine only synthesizes the reality that matters to the Person's current purpose, solving the combinatorial explosion through authored ontology rather than hidden engine code.
