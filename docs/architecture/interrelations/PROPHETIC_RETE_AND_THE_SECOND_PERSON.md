## The Interrelation

The introduction of multiplayer (`SECOND_PERSON_FRAMEWORK.md`) into a fundamentally permissive, law-driven ontology presents an immediate crisis: what happens when two Persons attempt mutually exclusive changes to the same `Object` at the exact same moment? If Person A's Law tries to destroy a relation while Person B's Law tries to reinforce it, who wins?

Traditional engines rely on authority servers or blind first-in-first-out transaction logs. Earthcall intertwines the **Prophetic Rete** and the **Hierarchy of Joys** to resolve this gracefully.

As detailed in `PROPHETIC_RETE.md`, the Rete network analyzes Laws *before* they fire. It already knows the exact property paths a Law intends to read and write. When a second Person enters the Zone, the Prophetic Rete can instantly identify intersecting "write intents" between the two Persons' active Laws.

When a conflict is mathematically inevitable, the system does not simply roll dice. It defers to the `HIERARCHY_OF_JOYS.md`. Because telos is explicitly ordered as rooted Formations of Lexemes (not just strings), the engine can compare the rank/telos of the two conflicting Laws or the Persons authoring them. The Rete network acts as a prophetic judge, identifying the collision ahead of time and using the established ontological order (the Hierarchy) to determine precedence before any state is actually corrupted.
