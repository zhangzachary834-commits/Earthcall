## Thoughts on Integration and Interrelation
The convergence of the Physics EventBus and Interaction as Law represents a critical milestone in Earthcall's No Black Box doctrine. By decoupling hard-wired physics mechanics from interaction responses, the EventBus allows physical collisions to become legible semantic facts that Law can directly reason over.

Rather than interactions being trapped within traditional UI or physics engine subsystems, any pointer event or physical collision resolves into an event on the EventBus. Both modalities share a unified semantic surface. This synthesis means a Person can author Laws that treat a mouse hover and a physical impact with the same vocabulary, making the simulation's underlying mathematics fundamentally authorable.
