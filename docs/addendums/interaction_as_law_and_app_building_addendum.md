# Addendum: Integrating Interaction as Law with Application Building

**Scope:** Architectural synthesis; not an end-to-end UI implementation certificate.

[Interaction as Law](../architecture/law/INTERACTION_AS_LAW.md) defines a control through an authored Object, a sensed interaction, and a Law that gives that interaction meaning. An `Activation::OnEvent` Law can respond to `object-clicked`; Conditions and ActionModels express the control's behavior. ObjectConcept and authored categories supply reuse without requiring a separate, authoritative `Button` class.

The [application-building guide](../architecture/Design/Building%202D%20and%203D%20Apps%20with%20Earthcall%20Guide.md) extends this principle from one control to a Zone-held ecology of Objects and other Singulars, Relations, Formations, Concepts, and Laws. Screen and other Singularity channels sense and manifest the authored structure; they do not decide its identity. A layout can be a Formation and laws over member positions rather than a compulsory widget tree.

**Implemented boundary:** The InteractionChannel sense path and first-mover control patterns are present, with headless tests documented in the interaction specification. That specification explicitly records an unrun manual protocol, while the application guide marks Person-facing authoring and granular pixel mastery as incomplete. The synthesis is a design method, not proof that a fully authored interface has been manually exercised.

**Design consequence:** Name the beings, authored Relations, relevant PropertyPaths, interaction events, and Laws first; then choose the modality that makes them observable. Preserve the Sense/Decide/Act and authorization boundaries rather than treating every pointer event as an unrestricted Law action.
