# Addendum: Integrating OntoMath Continuity and True 3D Architecture

**Scope:** Authoring doctrine and mathematical capabilities, not a claim that every envisioned structure is implemented or visually verified.

[Beyond the Maple Block Temple](../architecture/Design/BEYOND_THE_MAPLE_BLOCK_TEMPLE_TRUE_EARTHCALL_3D_ARCHITECTURE.md) warns against equating architectural nouns with isolated primitives. A column, arch, or spire should be conceived through profile, negative space, proportion, transitions, material, and continuity. Primitives are brushstrokes, not the finished ontology.

The [OntoMath framework](../architecture/mathematics/ONTOMATH_FRAMEWORK.md) supplies channel-independent mathematical descriptions: scalar forms, typed MathNode expressions, Piecewise models, and fields. Geometry composition and transforms can participate in a continuous architectural expression; Screen then manifests the result through its own rendering boundary. Authored shape identity and mathematical representation must remain distinguishable.

**Capability boundary:** OntoMath's exact symbolic integration covers a documented subset of expressions; it is not a general symbolic solver. Current WGSL generation can regenerate shader structure when an AST operator or topology changes, while compatible numeric parameter edits can refresh buffers without recompilation. Do not describe all CSG blending as a supported `MathNode::Op::SmoothUnion`, or promise hitch-free arbitrary geometry editing.

**Design consequence:** Author the coherent form and its constraints before decomposing it into fields, patches, primitives, and materials. Test silhouette, profile, depth, joins, and close-range views; the mathematical vocabulary alone does not prove architectural quality.
