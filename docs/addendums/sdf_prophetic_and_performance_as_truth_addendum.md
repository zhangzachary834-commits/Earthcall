# Addendum: Integrating SDF Prophetic and Performance as Truth

*(Model: Gemini 1.5 Pro, Harness: Jules, Session ID: 596534326296339974)*

## Reflections on the Architectural Synthesis

[Performance as Truth](../architecture/ontology/PERFORMANCE_AS_TRUTH.md) argues that Earthcall should seek speed by preserving and compiling the exact structure of authored truth rather than accumulating opaque approximations. The [SDF Spatial Prophetic Direct Profitability Artifact](../architecture/SDF_SPATIAL_PROPHETIC_DIRECT_PROFITABILITY_ARTIFACT.md) supplies a concrete falsification record for that principle.

### What the Artifact Actually Rejected

The SDF research did not prove that every spatial hierarchy or index is categorically invalid. It tested specific derived direct-dispatch representations against an exact authored-Perlin witness.

First, the positive-run representation was rejected for production. It preserved correctness but failed the deliberately hard profitability bar: reducing top-level queries concealed enough run-record discovery work that consultation economics remained poor.

Second, the corrected camera-independent stable geometric route atlas based on entry face, quantized entry coordinates, and direction classes was rejected. Finer geometric classification increased resident state and could increase runtime consequence work without reliably recovering the missing relevance relation.

Those are empirical boundaries on measured representation families, not a universal theorem against all spatial organization.

### The Governing Requirement

The artifact summarizes the lesson precisely:

> *A direct road is not "a smaller structure to search." It is a precompiled answer to which structure matters.*

The useful Prophetic consequences remained potentially profitable when relevance was already known. What failed was reconstructing that relevance at runtime through the tested external geometric classifications.

### The Next Research Rung

The document therefore proposes—not yet authorizes for production—a synthesized scene-spatial execution DAG. The hypothesis is to compile collective authored spatial semantics into an incrementally repairable execution/dependency DAG and attach Prophetic consequences to the exact compiled branches from which they derive.

That proposal still has a falsification boundary. A DAG that evaluates every leaf, or whose branch selection merely recreates generic spatial search, fails even if structurally elegant. A future test-only prototype must preserve exact generated-WGSL semantics, demonstrate zero hit mismatches, measure branch and leaf work, and prove dependency-frontier repair before production mutation.

### The Synthesis

The connection to Performance as Truth is therefore methodological rather than triumphalist. Earthcall measures candidate shadows instead of assuming they are cheaper, rejects representations whose hidden lookup work overwhelms their benefit, and keeps exact fallback semantics intact. Optimization earns architectural authority only when it preserves truth **and** demonstrates better execution economics.

The SDF artifact is valuable precisely because unsuccessful optimizations remain evidence: the positive-run and stable-route experiments narrow the search toward representations in which relevance is compiled from authored semantics instead of repeatedly rediscovered through the tested lookup families.
