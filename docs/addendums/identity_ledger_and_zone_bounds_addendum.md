## Thoughts on Integration and Interrelation

The relationship between how Identity is persisted and how Zones govern space reveals a fundamental principle of Earthcall's architecture: the strict separation of immutable truths from fluid, physical representations.

In *Succession Is Not In The World*, we see that the most critical continuity claim—mapping a name to a cryptographic key—is deliberately kept out of the shared world state. It resides in an unsigned, out-of-world ledger (`migration-ledger.json`). This ensures that identity succession (who someone truly is) cannot be spoofed, altered by in-world Laws, or compromised by spatial interactions. Identity is an external, unassailable anchor.

Conversely, *Zones As Mathematical Bounds* redefines Zones from arbitrary spatial containers into strict mathematical bounds (`Dimensional Zone`) within a continuum. A Person's location becomes merely a "reading"—a transient, derived state rather than an intrinsic property. A child Zone's object view inherently includes its parents', meaning physical presence is fluid and hierarchically layered.

**The Synthesis:**
When an entity moves through these mathematically bounded Zones, their spatial relationships and the Laws acting upon them shift dynamically. However, because their true Identity (succession) is anchored outside the world, spatial manipulation or Law-driven zone transitions can never strip or alter their fundamental existence or authority.

Furthermore, the strict rule in the Zones document—"Never write a save's bounds without its owner's authorization"—directly relies on the secure, out-of-world Identity ledger. Only the securely resolved identity can grant the cryptographic authorization required to permanently alter the boundaries of reality. Together, they form a system where physical reality is infinitely mutable through mathematics, but the souls traversing it are inviolable.
