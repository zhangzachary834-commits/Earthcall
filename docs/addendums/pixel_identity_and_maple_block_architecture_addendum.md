# Pixel Identity and the Maple Block Architecture Doctrine

**Status:** Architectural correspondence, not a mandatory identity mapping or implemented universal renderer.

Read [Pixels: substrate, process, and authored identity](../architecture/ontology/PIXEL_SUBSTRATE_PROCESS_AND_AUTHORED_IDENTITY.md) with [Beyond the Maple Block Temple](../architecture/Design/BEYOND_THE_MAPLE_BLOCK_TEMPLE_TRUE_EARTHCALL_3D_ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Primitives are brushstrokes, not architectural nouns

The Maple Block doctrine warns against allowing a renderer's primitive vocabulary to dictate the meaning or visible continuity of a cathedral, column, wall, or arch. A box can participate in a wall without becoming the definition of wall-ness. Better geometry comes from authored form, silhouette, voids, profiles, and coherent transitions, not merely multiplying blocks.

The pixel doctrine makes a related but **distinct** claim: a sample or region may be understood as accessible substrate, as the manifestation of an authored process, and/or as a vessel of authored identity. These perspectives can coexist. The doctrine does not require a Singular per pixel or primitive, a single Singular for an entire building, or a universal one-to-one mapping between mathematical nodes and identity-bearing beings.

## Where they meet

- **Substrate:** SDF leaves, transforms, fields, and rendered samples are mathematical or machine representations. They need not each carry an independent authored identity.
- **Process:** authored composition, carving, blending, and sampling can produce a visually coherent architectural whole. The act, its result, and the final observation are distinguishable.
- **Identity:** a Person may author identity for the building, a component, a region, or a mark using appropriate Singular bearers, Properties, and Relations. Identity does not arise automatically from primitive count or pixel coordinates.

A continuous-looking wall is not proof that all its contributing shapes share one Singular identity. Conversely, several meaningful architectural parts can retain distinct identities while presenting one coherent visual form. Neither doctrine grants a renderer authority to choose identity or collapse parts on the Person's behalf.

**Implementation boundary:** the pixel doctrine expressly leaves general substrate correspondence, temporal provenance, remapping, and automatic identity promotion open. The Maple Block guide is a design standard supported by visual experience, not a proof that every authored scene already meets it.

**Review questions:** does the result survive changes in viewpoint and lighting? Are primitive seams and profiles coherent? Are authored identities and their substrate locators distinguished? Does any claimed correspondence have explicit address, time, causation, and authority semantics?
