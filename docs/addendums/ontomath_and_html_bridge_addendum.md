## Reflections on the Architectural Synthesis

The [HTML Lexeme Formation Bridge](../architecture/Integration/HTML_LEXEME_FORMATION_BRIDGE.md) and [OntoMath Raster Formation and Property Graphs](../architecture/Design/ONTOMATH_RASTER_FORMATION_AND_PROPERTY_GRAPHS.md) can cooperate without collapsing sensing into manifestation. The bridge makes foreign structure legible; OntoMath is one possible authored way to give selected sensed structure spatial or visual form inside Earthcall.

### Sense Does Not Dictate Manifestation

Reading a foreign DOM does not require Earthcall to create a 3D Object, polygon mesh, collider, FaceTexture, SDF, or OntoMath field for every element. The foreign page remains the thing being sensed. Its Earthcall manifestation is a separate design decision.

A Person may author a manifestation that preserves useful semantic correspondences—for example, mapping selected element regions or raster-derived boundaries into continuous mathematical fields—but that choice belongs to Earthcall's authored layer rather than to the foreign source.

### OntoMath as One Authored Spatial Vocabulary

When a Person chooses an OntoMath-based manifestation:

1. **Visual matter may inform a field.** Raster-derived appearance can participate in a mathematical representation without declaring the raster itself to be the ontology.
2. **Semantic regions may correspond to mathematical regions.** Element bounds or other sensed structure can inform authored Piecewise/SDF-style regions where that representation is appropriate.
3. **Interaction remains Law-governed.** Laws may connect interaction with those authored regions back to the sensed semantic structure, without pretending that a foreign DOM intrinsically contains Earthcall geometry.

Other manifestations remain possible. The bridge therefore does not prescribe OntoMath; it supplies legible foreign facts from which Persons can author an OntoMath manifestation when that serves their design.

### Conclusion

The HTML Bridge answers **what foreign structure Earthcall can sense and name**. OntoMath can answer **how a Person chooses to manifest some of that structure mathematically**. Preserving that seam keeps foreign applications from becoming black boxes while also refusing the opposite error: allowing foreign data to dictate Earthcall's ontology or spatial form.
