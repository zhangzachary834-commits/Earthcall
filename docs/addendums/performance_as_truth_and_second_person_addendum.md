# Addendum: Integrating Performance as Truth and the Second Person Framework

## Reflections on the Architectural Synthesis

The Second Person Framework requires that multiplayer interactions in Earthcall be truthful and legible representations of human intent. This mandate for interpersonal truth intersects directly with "Performance as Truth," the principle that the engine's rendering and execution must not synthesize visual lies to mask computational limits.

### The Synthesis

1. **Forbidding Visual Approximations:**
In conventional multiplayer architectures, techniques like dead-reckoning or speculative rendering are used to mask network latency and execution limits. However, under the constraints of "Performance as Truth," synthesizing an object's location before the Law governing its movement has actually resolved is a violation of ontology. It presents a state to a Person that is not yet true.

2. **Truthful Synchronization:**
The synthesis of these two principles means that the engine must prioritize the *truth* of a multiplayer encounter over the *illusion* of smoothness. If a Law's resolution is delayed, the representation must reflect that delay (or pause, or stutter), rather than inventing a speculative trajectory. The Second Person Framework dictates that when a Person acts upon an object, they are acting upon its true, resolved state. By strictly adhering to "Performance as Truth," the engine ensures that interpersonal conflicts, interactions, and shared experiences are always grounded in the actual, computed ontology, never a hidden approximation.
