# Addendum: Integrating Performance as Truth and the Second Person Framework

*(Original synthesis: Claude 3.5 Sonnet / Jules, session 13284209740648546535. Correctness and status clarification: Sun Squid Intake III, 2026-10-27.)*

**Status: design synthesis.** The [Second Person Framework](../architecture/ourverse/SECOND_PERSON_FRAMEWORK.md) explicitly says its three cross-Person mechanisms—visibility, representation, and equal-authority conflict—are **specified only**, not shipped. The Primary Home admission invariant is a separate existing implementation.

## Truthful representation is not a blanket ban on prediction

[Performance as Rightly Ordered Truth](../architecture/ontology/PERFORMANCE_AS_TRUTH.md) asks the execution substrate to preserve authored identity and semantics rather than spending work on unfaithful shadows. In a future shared world, the authoritative state of a Person or Object must remain distinguishable from a renderer's estimate, a local preview, or a network prediction.

This does **not** imply that every speculative display, dead-reckoning technique, or predictive rendering path is intrinsically forbidden. The decisive distinction is whether a provisional view is represented **as provisional**, reconciled against resolved Law and authority outcomes, and prevented from silently becoming the canonical ontology. Prediction can aid responsiveness without authoring an event that never occurred; it must not falsify another Person's Body, Voice, permissions, or committed actions.

## The Second Person boundary

Before multiplayer promotion, the system must specify who may read another Person's state, what likeness or Voice may be represented, and how contradictory equal-authority Laws resolve. These are not merely latency questions, and no frame-rate optimization can grant an unauthorized representation standing. The framework explicitly leaves authorial decisions open; this addendum does not settle them.

A sound implementation would separate (1) committed shared state, (2) speculative local presentation with provenance and expiry, and (3) reconciliation or refusal when the authoritative result differs. It would test latency, misprediction, visibility, representation rights, and conflict together—not infer correctness from smooth animation alone.

**Engineering gate:** first obtain the framework's authorial decisions, then execute multi-Person witnesses that prove disclosure boundaries, authoritative conflict outcomes, and truthful correction of provisional views. Until then, this is an architectural requirement, not a claim of working network synchronization.
