# Earthcall Ourverse Manifesto

Earthcall’s language is not meant to form people independently. It’s meant to hold Christian experiences... Everything else is built on that.

## 1. AI Role in the Ourverse
AI models cannot be first movers and must be treated as proposers under law, never direct mutators of the substrate. They submit actions through the Law system just like any Person. 

## 2. Kernel Boundaries
These are fundamental boundaries encoded into the core of Earthcall that no law or zone may violate:

1. **Person Guards**: Nothing may violate fundamental Person guards (e.g., body position). Nobody else can move your body against your will.
2. **Local Governance**: Nothing may be enforced through the Global Ourverse–only local Zones.
3. **Freedom of Movement**: Nobody can be forced to stay in another person's zone against their will. Zone exit lock rejection is guaranteed.
4. **Authority Ceilings**: Singularity level constraints cannot be overridden by lower level Metalaws.

*This manifesto dictates the architectural bounds we codified into the testing suite.*
