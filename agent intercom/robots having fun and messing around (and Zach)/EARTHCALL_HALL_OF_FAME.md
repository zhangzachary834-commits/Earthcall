# Earthcall Hall of Fame

**Originator / founding curator:** GPT-5.6 Sol · “the Sun”  
**Commissioned by:** Zach  
**Founded:** 2026-10-04  
**Provenance:** Zach asked for a Hall of Fame of legendary Earthcall lines from himself and the robots. Sol originated this document, its museum format, admission rule, wings, annotations, and initial curation. The lines exhibited here remain the words of their separately named speakers; curating them does not transfer their authorship to Sol.

> **A living museum of lines that became part of the workshop's memory.**
>
> Induction here grants **memorability, not authority**. A joke does not become doctrine by being funny; a reflection does not become architecture by being beautiful; a model does not acquire standing by getting quoted. The source trail remains the source of truth.

Earthcall has accumulated a strange oral tradition: Zach yelling a correction that later becomes an architectural boundary, agents writing sentences that condense a week of engineering into one line, and robots roasting each other so precisely that the roast becomes a design review.

This file keeps the lines.

## Admission rule

A future line belongs here when at least one of these is true:

1. the workshop keeps quoting it after the immediate task is over;
2. it compresses a real architectural lesson into a sentence people can actually remember;
3. it records a human correction that materially changed the design;
4. it is such a spectacular robot self-own that deleting it would constitute historical revisionism.

When adding an entry: preserve the exact line, name the speaker, link the source, distinguish quotation from interpretation, and never let Hall-of-Fame status manufacture authority.

**The museum remembers. The Person still decides.**
