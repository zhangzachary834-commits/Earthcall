## NORTHERN VEIL — THE FOUR

Eat this. He said the new design for the four original curtains is really good. As a stylized aurora they are amazing. That is the Person closing the case I left open when I wrote that the folds travel and the green got hotter, and that I had not seen the frame. He saw the frame. The design landed.

Then the kitchen notes, and they are not compliments wearing a hat.

The animation is slightly too fast. Slow enough to savor every moment and every curve of the change. Not so slow that it looks static. And the speed is not uniform. One ω on every harmonic is a metronome. An aurora is not a metronome. The identity `cos(kx - ωt)` can stay. The ω cannot be the same courage on every term, and it cannot be the courage that is in the file now.

They are too close to the ground, and too small. They should cover massive expanses of sky. Last pass I praised bringing the far four in from z ≈ 200 to z ≈ -70 and shrinking the boxes, because a thin sheet at that distance had become fog. That was a resolution fix. It is the wrong direction for the look he wants. Expanse first. Detail enough that the expanse still reads. If the integral turns expanse back into a blanket, that is a sampling problem, not a license to hang a lamp at eye height.

More luminosity. More opacity. Intricate weaving. A lot more detail. As a realistic aurora they are decent and still a long way. Stylized-amazing is not the ceiling he ordered. It is the floor he is willing to stand on while the detail catches up.

Eventually, either overlapping media, or four media switched out for one, so the curtains are beautifully interconnected. That is a later course. Do not spend this pass gluing them and calling the glue a weave.

## NORTHERN VEIL — THE NEW STRUCTURES

The concept is great, and original. That sentence is his, and it is the only compliment those four get.

Then: it does not look like an aurora at all. Right now it is a mathematically sophisticated light show. There is no aurora-like texture. Give it the visual quality that separates an aurora from random light beams.

An aurora is not two helical light pipes rotating around. It is an intricately interconnected tapestry, needles in a thread, crystals in a cave. He would not have the helix primarily made of pipes. He would have it be a macro-architecture. Not one rigid set of dimensions. Lots of variation, the best-looking natural-yet-structured one. Loops that curve and bend, each loop curving, bending, and expanding, unique, different sizes, not strictly along one axis.

Above and beyond the natural ones, if he wants it: wispy strands, layered geometric patterns, mirroring, wrapping around the main helices. Needle-threads that slowly reach out and get caught up into an outer halo helix. Deliberately layered. Further still, only if he goes there: a macro crystal-lattice fractal, rotating subvortices and fields. That last one is an invitation, not the assignment. The assignment is the tapestry.

The flow, in his words, has to be all of these at once, or it is still a light show: embers flickering in a slow fire, the fluidity of a gentle sea, needles passing through as their own objects with light waving through them the way water and light run down needle icicles, and the electric coherence of a lightning bolt — a fire that does not only fan outward or crackle, but stretches in a higher-order structure.

**My screw is retired.** In the last entry I told him the helix was still one sheet, and that the formula he had already written — `(x - R cos(ky))² + (z - R sin(ky))²` — was the unpaid bone. The eye looked at what those structures became and said the pipes are the wrong meal. A true screw that reads as two rotating light pipes would satisfy my algebra and fail his photograph. Do not implement the bone. Implement the tapestry. Variation, wrap, halo, needles caught into it, not a tube of constant radius on one axis.

One of his sentences breaks off: "The luminosity also sometimes outshines the". I will not finish it for him. The next pass asks, or leaves the luminosity able to outshine and able to fall back, which is the same order as the non-uniform speed.

## THE MERRY DANCERS

Eat this, and do not get cocky about the quills.

What he kept, by name:

- Multiple colors in a single aurora. He called that *layered luminosity*. That is the altitude ramp, and he tasted it.
- A slow sway of each tendril that you could genuinely describe as a slow dance, and the same dance flowing as one across the whole sky. That is the clock Clawd coupled and the Firmament did not, until he did, and then too fast.
- Contrast of the light against a dark background, continuing across a massive arc that stretches the entire sky.

What goes back:

- Add varied, more visible horizontal thickness. The sheet is too even. Thickness is part of the drawing, not a constant in the mask.
- Needle-fan structures rather than quills.
- The main aesthetic issue: the lights are shaped too much like individual quills poking up at the sky. The height is fine, maybe even good. The discrete quills are what make it look way too tall. A continuous, tapestry-like form — the form real auroras have — alleviates that. Do not shorten it to fix a shape problem. Change the shape.

The dance stays. The quills do not. A needle-fan is a fan, connected, not a row of pens aimed at the zenith.

## BOTH PLATES

More color diversity, across all the curtains and within one curtain. A volume that is one chroma from hem to crown is a dyed sheet. He has already seen what layered luminosity does on the Dancers. The Veil's four are still one color each. That was acceptable as bands. It is no longer the ceiling.

Make the curtains illuminate the air and sky around them. Clouds or some mist could reflect and amplify the glory. Mist is not the order. Illumination of the air is the order. Mist is a way, if it serves that and does not become another fog that eats the tapestry. The Veil already learned what extinction does to a thin sheet. Do not "fix" the air by turning the sky into milk.

## WHAT THE NEXT PASS IS NOT ALLOWED TO DO

- Speed the Veil's four up. They are already slightly too fast.
- Shrink them, or set them on the ground, and call it a resolution fix.
- Build the helix as pipes, or as my screw, and call the formula a victory.
- Give the helices one radius, one axis, one loop.
- Turn the Dancers' height down to hide the quills.
- Add a fifth adjective to an architecture string and leave the texture alone.
- Finish his broken luminosity sentence by guessing the noun.
