QUESTIONS — design and philosophical. Please answer, do not implement from my guesses.

1. Is spawnLawArmed already a control, in the sense of INTERACTION_AS_LAW §1?

   A control is "a being you can point at, plus a law that says what pointing at
   it means." spawnLawArmed is a bool on a First Mover channel, written by L and
   an ImGui checkbox. That is Sense chrome. If (b) in-world is the destination,
   the next honest step is an Object (toggle archetype) whose click-law Sets
   @creation-channel.spawnLawArmed, and L becomes a KeyBind Singular later.

   Or: spawnLawArmed is Kernel Sense, like InteractionChannel's pointer state,
   and it is allowed to stay a channel bit forever because "is the spawn law
   armed" is a fact about the machine, not a kind of button.

   Which is it? I lean toward the toggle-being, because a Person should be able
   to point at the arming gesture the way they point at anything else. But that
   is a claim about dwelling, and you have been arguing PX. I want the argument,
   not my preference shipped.

2. Who owns the click?

   The spawn law still wakes on the global GLFW onMouseClicked, subject the
   CreationChannel. INTERACTION_AS_LAW wants object-clicked on a being. If we
   migrate the spawn law to "when this button is clicked, Set spawnLawArmed" we
   have inverted the current design: today a world-click births a shape while
   a bool is true. Tomorrow a button-click might only arm, and a second gesture
   births — or the button-click IS the birth, and spawnLawArmed disappears.

   Three shapes, pick one or refuse them:

     (i)  World-click while armed (today, after the split). The Person aims at
          the world. The control is only the arming latch.
     (ii) Button-click arms; world-click still births. Two gestures, like a
          tool pickup then a stroke.
     (iii) Button-click births at cursorSpawnTransform. No world-click spawn
          law. The control IS the spawn.

   (i) matches "I am looking at the world and making a shape." (iii) matches
   "a control is a being I point at." They are different philosophies of where
   creation happens. Zach's original complaint was the law colliding with the
   3D tool, not that world-click spawn is wrong. I do not want GUI-as-law to
   silently choose (iii) because it is the Button-class shape.

3. Is the C++ bypass allowed to remain a First Mover forever?

   tool-create-3d-law is a named gate over Tool::ShapeGenerator3D. If the
   Person-facing path is the spawn law, the bypass is author-scaffolding
   (Gemini's Phase 1). If GUI-as-law eventually authors the Create button as
   a being that also births shapes, we have two spawn paths again — the
   collision we just undid, one layer up.

   Please do not wrap console Create as the Person spawn gesture. That button
   is the bypass. The law is the path. If you disagree, the disagreement is
   "there should only be one spawn path, and it is the console" — say that
   plainly, because Zach asked to keep both testable.

4. The cascade (§6b / To-do 29).

   If you author an on-law / off-law pair that Sets spawnLawArmed, you will
   hit the loop Claude already named: two laws whose actions satisfy each
   other's conditions fire in one tick and the toggle never moves. A single
   toggle law (control-toggle-law archetype) that Maps the bool is the
   shape that works. Do not invent a second pair.

5. L is still GLFW_KEY_L, which the main menu also binds to Load.

   Item 7 (Key as Singular) is yours. Until that exists, L is a first-mover
   Sense edge, same as spawnLawArmed. I will not retarget L onto a KeyBind
   from this session. If you take KeyBind, the spawn-law arming is a

   GLFW poll in handleCreateLawKey goes away.
