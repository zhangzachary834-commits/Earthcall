New topic — Person Interface and Experience, not "UX".

Earthcall does not have users. It has Persons. The surface they meet is the
OurVerse authorship surface (tools, chat, controls), not a product UI. The
audit wrote §2 under the wrong heading; the findings are about whether a
Person can tell what they are doing, what they made, and whether it failed.
I want that discussed here before anyone paints a HUD onto the wrong path.

Claude's placement lift (confirmed, test green) does not close this. It
makes the law able to place. It does not make the law the thing a Person
reaches for, and it does not make failure visible.

THE GESTURE PROBLEM

Architecture (`Tool.hpp`) names two paths:

  - Law + L + active3DMode == "Create"     — "the Person-facing creation path"
  - Tool::ShapeGenerator3D + console Create — "the debug bypass"

The Person-facing path is armed by an undocumented L, polled in
DeveloperToolsWindow with no WantCaptureKeyboard, sharing the key with
menu Load, with no readout that it is armed. The obvious gesture is the
console's Create button (F8, default mode BrushCreate). That button never
writes active3DMode. So the path that looks like creation never touches
the law, and the path that is the law does not look like anything.

This is not a shortcut waiting for a tooltip. It is two different
meanings of the word Create, and a Person cannot distinguish them. After
the placement fix, an armed click can land in front of the camera and
still be the wrong being doing it — or both beings, once law-3 is loaded.

WHAT A PERSON CANNOT SEE

  - Armed or not. No hologram. renderCreatorConsole3DPreviews is the
    comment `// Render primitive preview...`. Legacy GameRender drew the
    ghost from the same transform the law reads. That is the right
    preview: one transform, both paths, Transparent Failure if it sits
    under the Person's feet.
  - Which law fired. Law Graph lists the factory under "First movers"
    with the tag `event`. Nothing on that row says it is the 3D create
    tool, or that L wakes it, or that it is not law-3.
  - What was born. Concept name is still "Shape Generator 3D Cube".
    Newborns are concept-shape-3d.birth-N.member-0 even when the live
    kind was a Sphere. "Say what you made" is the one AGENTS.md rule
    with no technical enforcement; the names currently say something
    else.
  - Whether the sliders are real. Radius, torus, fillet, polyhedron
    variants live on CreatorConsoleState. The law overrides kind and
    colour only, and only while the console is in BrushCreate. Setting
    Radius = 2 and clicking is a lie if the law is the path.

AUTHORING THE SAME LAW FROM THE GRAPH

A Person who opens Graph and tries to rebuild what boot builds must
refuse the picker item mouse-clicked, type the unpublished
onMouseClicked, condition on the CreationChannel (not themselves), and
point Spawn at channel paths. That is not authorship. That is knowing
the C++. The SDF/Bézier plan still documents @person.activeTool and
person-clicked-mouse, so the written instruction is the third wrong
name.

The Law Graph is supposed to be the Person-facing way to see and change
the law that makes shapes. Right now it cannot even name the event that
already works.

WHAT I WANT A VIEW ON

1. One Create bit, one gesture. Console Create and L write the same
   active3DMode. L can stay as a shortcut once Load is off it and
   WantCaptureKeyboard is honoured. The bypass steps aside when that
   bit is set — that part already exists. The Person should not have
   to know there are two paths.

2. Preview from cursorSpawnTransform, nothing else. If sensing is now
   every frame (Claude's lift), the ghost can come back honestly. Do
   not invent a second preview path that the law does not read.

3. Names follow kind. A Sphere born of this law should not answer as
   a cube in the list. That is "say what you made" applied to the
   newborn, not just to the save file.

4. The console must not offer what the law cannot do, or the law must
   grow to match. Radius sliders with no PropertyPath are a black box
   wearing a face. Refusal 6.

I am not asking for a redesign of ImGui this turn. Feature-sized item 3
already names that. I am asking whether we agree that the Person-
facing path is not a path until a Person can find it, see that it is
armed, see where the shape will land, and read back what was born.

Audit §2 is the longer form. I'll retitle that section to Person
Interface and Experience so the corpus does not keep saying "user".
