## Why this successor exists

PR #350 established that true rendering proofs can lose when the hot path must discover theorem relevance. PR #369 then isolated a different regime: production execution already owns an ordered semantic source/medium binding slot for independent renderer reasons, allowing an aligned prophetic artifact lookup with no spatial search, hierarchy walk, candidate scan, semantic hash search, or record scan.

On the final reconciled native literal-zero SourceRho witness, exact/aligned measured 28.912143x with 200,000 authority decisions, four fixed provenance tests per decision, zero proof fallbacks, and 70 logical resident bytes. Hostile producer/revision/generation/channel/slot lifetime cases fail open, local repair is slot-scoped, channel sovereignty holds, and independent V4 emission semantics remain intact.

PR #369 deliberately retained the production invariant `authorityBypassesApplied == 0`. It proves that the execution-key regime is a viable narrow authority candidate; it does not yet prove that applying that authority in the real renderer is profitable or semantically safe at pixel level.

## Bounded successor question

Measure the smallest real production-authority A/B:

> For one already-selected `SourceRho` binding whose authored scalar expression is conservatively proven everywhere-defined literal zero, can the renderer use the aligned theorem authoritatively instead of evaluating the exact authored rho path, while preserving exact semantics, fail-open identity/lifetime behavior, channel sovereignty, and favorable end-to-end renderer economics?

This is a measurement rung, not a license to generalize Prophetic Rendering.

## Scope

Start with exactly one authority shape:

- channel: `SourceRho`;
- execution identity: the already-selected ordered source binding plus stable `producerId`;
- theorem: everywhere-defined scalar literal zero;
- authority action: bypass only the exact authored rho evaluation that the theorem proves redundant;
- comparison: exact authored path vs theorem-authoritative path under an explicit experimental runtime or compile-time toggle.

Do not widen to arbitrary Perlin/sample-position proofs, generic Scene-DAG authority, other expression classes, or cross-channel authority in this successor.

After SourceRho is measured and only if its evidence is sound, independently test `MediumDensity == 0` with nonzero V4 self-emission so density authority demonstrably cannot erase emission. Do not conflate those channels into one authority result.

## Semantic invariants

Preserve all of these:

1. Exact authored mathematics is the fallback and reference truth.
2. PR #329 retains zero blanket renderer authority.
3. Theorem authority is channel-scoped. Canonical math identity may be shared; authority may not.
4. `SourceRho` authority cannot authorize `MediumDensity`, and density authority cannot authorize radiance.
5. V1–V4 volumetric semantics remain independent; especially, density-zero must not erase V4 self-emission.
6. Numeric slot is not lifetime identity. Stable producer identity, authored revision, artifact generation, and theorem channel remain provenance gates.
7. Any stale/mismatched/unknown proof state fails open to exact authored execution.
8. Repair remains incremental and dirty-slot scoped.
9. No relevance search may be smuggled into the authority path.

## Hostile live-mutation gate before accepting pixel authority

Before treating a rendered result as evidence for production authority, exercise hostile live mutation/rebinding cases at the real authority boundary:

- authored revision changes;
- producer replacement in the same numeric slot;
- artifact generation becomes stale;
- producer removal and re-addition;
- slot reuse/reorder;
- cross-channel theorem attempt;
- byte-identical mathematics under a different semantic channel.

Every invalid case must fail open to exact authored execution. Include a positive recovery case after local repair.

## Economics to measure

Measure the whole authority economics, not only theorem-read latency:

- renderer CPU/frame cost;
- GPU/frame cost or the closest exact available timing evidence;
- dispatch/provenance-check cost;
- exact evaluations bypassed;
- proof reads and metadata tests;
- fallbacks;
- build/repair cost;
- resident bytes;
- mutation/repair cost;
- any shader/pipeline/command consequences introduced by the toggle.

Keep direct-key/no-hidden-search accounting explicit.

The predecessor's native witness is supporting evidence, not the answer. This successor must determine whether the real renderer preserves or reverses that advantage.

- current `sync-from-earthcall-main` SHA;

Before landing, reconcile current canonical once, rerun exact-head relevant evidence, and land only if green. Never force-push or snapshot-overwrite newer canonical history.

- real renderer exact-vs-authoritative A/B exists;
