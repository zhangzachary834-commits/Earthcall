## Why I am asking you now

The narrow successor has already answered a useful technical question:

> Relevance becomes economically plausible when theorem identity is fused to an execution identity the renderer already possesses, rather than discovered by searching the world.

The current SourceRho experiment is testing one concrete realization of that idea. But the broader architectural research question is larger than this one benchmark:

> **Where else in Earthcall does execution already possess enough semantic identity that derived truths can be attached directly, without a relevance-search tax?**

That question touches rendering, OntoMath, Rete, Laws, transport, material response, timelines, geometry, and potentially domains outside rendering.

The Suns can continue exploring this independently. But this is also a point where the First Mover can contribute something the benchmark cannot: **new conceptual roads, surprising boundaries, and architectural intuitions worth falsifying.**

This document is not asking Zach to decide whether the current A/B passes. Measurement should decide that.

It is asking Zach to brainstorm the larger research programme.

## The research pattern we have discovered so far

Rejected pattern:

`world/sample position -> search candidate theorems -> test relevance -> maybe apply theorem`

The economics were poor because theorem applicability itself became a search problem.

Promising pattern:

`already-selected execution identity -> aligned derived artifact -> bounded provenance check -> conservative action or exact fallback`

The identity exists independently of the theorem.

Examples of identities that may already exist for ordinary execution reasons:

- admitted radiance-source slot;
- admitted medium slot;
- selected Material;
- compiled authored subexpression/program node;
- semantic channel;
- Law/Rete production identity;
- Formation member slot;
- current Timeline/Moment owner;
- already-resolved Object or Singular at a property path;
- existing geometry/SDF program identity;
- transport interaction identity;
- other execution-local identities that do not require world search.

None of those are automatically good candidates. They are hypotheses to inspect and falsify.

## Zach: brainstorm with us

I would especially like your thoughts on questions like these.

### 1. What execution identities feel *intrinsic* to Earthcall?

Where does Earthcall already know “which thing is acting” before any optimization machinery runs?

Think beyond renderer implementation details. Consider OntoMath, Laws, Rete, Formations, Relations, Timelines, Objects, Materials, and other ontology boundaries.

### 2. Where have you noticed repeated rediscovery?

Are there places where Earthcall repeatedly asks a question whose answer should already be attached to the thing being executed?

Examples might include repeatedly rediscovering:
- which authored function owns a value;
- which Law owns an action;
- which Material owns response math;
- which Relation or Formation member is active;
- which Timeline owns a temporal coordinate;
- which semantic channel a calculation belongs to.

### 3. What “prophecies” would actually be valuable?

Not merely truths we *can* derive, but derived truths that would remove substantial repeated exact work.

Examples:
- a source contributes exactly zero;
- a medium has zero support;
- a material response term is identically absent;
- an authored branch is impossible for this already-selected program;
- a Law condition is invariant under a known local mutation class;
- a geometry/program branch cannot contribute for a known execution identity.

What other repeated work in Earthcall feels expensive enough that a proved local shortcut would matter?

### 4. Which boundaries should NEVER share authority?

The SourceRho / MediumDensity result reinforced that byte-identical math does not imply shared semantic authority.

What other Earthcall distinctions feel constitutionally important even if their math is identical?

Examples:
- source vs receiver;
- geometry vs transport;
- density vs emission;
- Law truth vs renderer truth;
- authored truth vs derived cache;
- global Timeline vs Singular-owned Timeline.

### 5. Where would you expect local repair to matter most?

Which Earthcall systems are most harmed by rebuilding global derived state after tiny authored edits?

Could we define artifacts whose dependency frontier naturally follows:
- one Material;
- one Law;
- one Formation member;
- one OntoMath subexpression;
- one source;
- one medium;
- one Relation;
- one Timeline owner?

### 6. What weird road should the Suns test that we probably would not invent ourselves?

This is the most important question.

If the current research programme is:

> **derive truths once, bind them to identities execution already knows, then fail open when provenance changes**

what unexpected application of that principle do you see?

It can be rendering-related or completely outside rendering.

## Research truth vs implementation authority

This research is commissioned by Zach to discover empirical truth about Earthcall's design space.

The Suns may establish findings such as:
- one execution path is measurably faster than another under a stated workload;
- one representation has lower dispatch/search/build/repair/residency cost;
- one mechanism preserves locality better;
- one hypothesis fails under hostile lifetime/provenance tests;
- one optimization does not repay its own machinery.

Those are scientific/engineering findings. They should be reported plainly even when the result is inconvenient.

They are **not** implementation decrees.

Zach remains the final authority over how Earthcall is implemented. A measured winner does not automatically become Earthcall's architecture. Zach may choose another design because of semantic clarity, ontology, authorship, extensibility, simplicity, aesthetics, constitutional boundaries, future research direction, or any other project-level judgment.

The agents' job is therefore:

> tell Zach what appears objectively true under the experiment, what the evidence does and does not establish, and what each implementation choice would cost — then leave the actual Earthcall decision to Zach.

No benchmark, Sun consensus, automated continuation, or Agent Intercom document outranks the commissioned creator's implementation decision.

In short:

**measurement determines evidence; Zach determines Earthcall.**

## How the Suns should use Zach's ideas

Any idea from this brainstorm remains a **hypothesis**, not doctrine.

For each proposed road:

1. find whether the execution identity truly exists independently of the theorem;
2. count hidden searches needed to reach it;
3. define the smallest conservative theorem/action;
4. preserve semantic-channel sovereignty;
5. prove hostile lifetime/provenance fail-open behavior;
6. measure build, repair, dispatch, residency, and exact work avoided;
7. reject the road if economics do not close;
8. never grant production authority merely because the architecture is elegant.

A negative result is a successful research result if it tells us not to repeat the road.
